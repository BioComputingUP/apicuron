APICURON

Credit for the work that keeps science running.

Publications and their metrics capture only part of research. Much of the work that sustains it goes unrecognised: curated data, research software, training materials and more. The NTO Registry calls these non-traditional research outputs (NTOs). APICURON collects contribution events from partner resources, currently covering data curation, research software and training. It turns them into statistics, achievements and leaderboards, and gives every contributor a public profile they can share.
