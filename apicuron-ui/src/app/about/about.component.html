Funding

APICURON is currently supported by the University of Padova, which provides the necessary hardware infrastructure and personnel for software maintenance, both via its core funding and ELIXIR-Italy funding. In addition, APICURON has received support from ELIXIR's Data Platform, which has provided funding to improve integration with core data resources and to enhance guidelines and standardization processes for connecting resources.

To ensure the long-term sustainability of APICURON, we plan to pursue additional funding opportunities from both public and private sources. We will also explore partnerships with relevant organizations and stakeholders to increase the visibility and impact of the platform.

Additionally, we plan to explore potential revenue streams, such as charging for premium features or offering consulting services, to supplement existing funding sources; especially for private entities such as pharmaceutical companies building their private datasets. Finally, we will continue to prioritize sustainable software development practices to ensure that the platform remains stable, secure, and efficient.

QA life cycle plan

The Quality Assurance Life Cycle Plan defines the framework for maintaining and continuously improving the quality, reliability, and accuracy of APICURON. It establishes the Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs), implementation roadmap, resource improvement plan, and Quality Assurance (QA) and Quality Control (QC) practices required to ensure the database meets its intended objectives.

The plan supports the consistent application of quality standards through regular reviews, internal audits, and continuous improvement activities. It also provides a framework for integrating future preservation policies to support the long-term integrity, accessibility, and sustainability of APICURON.

Scientific Advisory Board

APICURON does not currently have a separate Scientific Advisory Board (SAB), it is reviewed through the SAB of the Italian ELIXIR node. Moreover, it is involved in the ELIXIR Biocuration Focus Group and linked to the International Society for Biocuration (ISB). These connections to the broader biocuration community provide valuable guidance and feedback to help ensure that the platform operates in accordance with best practices and standards.

ELSI policy and equal opportunity

APICURON is committed to upholding ethical, legal, and social implications (ELSI) policies and providing equal opportunity research support. These principles ensure that the resource operates in a fair and inclusive manner, taking into account the ethical considerations and promoting equal access and opportunity for all users and researchers.

ELSI policy

  • Data Privacy and Consent: APICURON adheres to strict data privacy guidelines and respects user consent. Any personal or sensitive data collected is handled securely and in compliance with applicable data protection regulations.
  • Ethical Use of Data: The resource promotes the ethical use of data and encourages collaborators and curators to consider the potential implications and societal impact of their work. Guidelines are provided to ensure that data usage aligns with ethical standards and legal requirements.
  • Responsible Data Sharing: APICURON encourages responsible data sharing practices, promoting open access while respecting intellectual property rights and providing proper attribution to data sources.
  • Bias Mitigation: Efforts are made to mitigate biases in data collection, analysis, and representation. Steps are taken to ensure that the database content is diverse, inclusive, and representative of different populations and research domains.

Equal opportunity research support

  • Accessibility: APICURON strives to provide an accessible platform for users with disabilities. Efforts are made to ensure compatibility with assistive technologies and compliance with web accessibility standards.
  • User Support and Training: APICURON provides user support and training resources to assist researchers in utilizing the resource effectively. This support is offered to users regardless of their background, institution, or research experience, ensuring equal opportunity for all.
  • Collaboration and Networking Opportunities: The resource promotes collaboration and networking among researchers, fostering an environment where individuals from different backgrounds can connect, share knowledge, and engage in interdisciplinary research.

Diversity and inclusion

  • Representation: APICURON values diversity and inclusion and strives to ensure representation across various dimensions, including gender, ethnicity, geography, and research fields. Efforts are made to include underrepresented groups and promote balanced representation in the resource's content.
  • Sensitivity and Cultural Considerations: The resource recognizes the importance of cultural sensitivity in research and content representation. Guidelines are provided to address cultural considerations and avoid any form of discrimination or misrepresentation.
  • Equal Opportunity for Contributions: APICURON encourages scientific or biological databases from all backgrounds to contribute and benefit from APICURON as a platform that promotes fairness and equal opportunity, providing a level playing field for curators and various contributors to showcase their work and contribute to the advancement of knowledge in biology and science.

By implementing an ELSI policy and providing equal opportunity research support, APICURON aims to create a welcoming and inclusive environment for users, researchers and institutions. These principles ensure that the resource operates ethically, respects user privacy, and provides equal opportunities for researchers to engage, contribute, and benefit from the resources and knowledge available on the platform.
