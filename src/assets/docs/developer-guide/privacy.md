# APICURON Privacy Policy

This privacy notice explains what personal data is collected by APICURON, for what purposes,
how it is processed, and how we keep it secure.

## What data do we collect?

Our Company collects the following data:
- Personal identification information (Name, email address, ORCID id)
- User activity

## How do we collect your data?

You directly provide APICURON with most of the data we collect. We collect data and process
data when you:
- Create an account and register through ORCID for our products or services.
- Login and voluntarily opt-in to share your ORCID profile and information with us.

## How will we use your data?

We will use the personal data to:
 - Provide user access to the service
 - Create anonymous usage statistics
 - Provide requested information, services and support

## How do we store your data?

APICURON securely stores your data on our servers.

## How long do we store your personal data?

Any personal data directly obtained from you will be retained as long as the service is live, even if
you stop using the service. We will keep the personal data for the minimum amount of time
possible to ensure legal compliance and to facilitate internal and external audits if they arise.
Access to the web and security logs containing personal data are restricted to relevant and
authorised APICURON staff.

## Will your personal data be transferred to third countries (i.e. countries not part of

## EU/EAA) and/or international organisations?

Personal data is transferred to the following service provider based in a third country which
APICURON relies on to provide the service:


 - Google Analytics

There are no personal data transfers to international organisations.


## What are your data protection rights?

In compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), every user is entitled to
the following:

1. The right to not be subject to decisions based solely on an automated
    processing of data (i.e. without human intervention) without you having your views
    taken into consideration.
2. The right to access - You have the right to request APICURON for copies of your
    personal data at reasonable intervals. Under your request we will inform you in writing
    about, for example, the origin of the personal data or the preservation period.
3. **The right to be informed –** You have the right to request information to understand data processing
    activities when the results of these activities are applied to you.
4. The right to object to processing - You have the right to object to APICURON processing of your
    personal data, under certain conditions.
5. The right to rectification - You have the right to request that APICURON correct any
    information you believe is inaccurate. You also have the right to request APICURON to
    complete information you believe is incomplete.
6. The right to erasure - You have the right to request that APICURON erase your
    personal data, under certain conditions.

It must be clarified that rights 4, 5 and 6 are only available whenever the processing of your personal
data is not necessary to:

 - Comply with a legal obligation.
 - Perform a task carried out in the public interest.
 - Exercise authority as a data controller.
 - Archive for purposes in the public interest, or for historical research purposes, or for
statistical purposes.
 - Establish, exercise or defend legal claims.


If you make a request, we have one month to respond to you.
