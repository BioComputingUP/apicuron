Partner Resources

This page lists all member databases registered to APICURON. Member databases periodically push curation activities in APICURON. By clicking on the "See details" button you can visit database pages where you can find the leaderboard, the achievements definition (badges, medals) and the full list of curation records.

Warning

A comparison across databases is meaningless . Different curation databases evaluate and weight curation activities differently. Each database defines curation activities at different granularity. The scores assigned to curation activities may have different meanings in different databases. For some databases scores are proportional to the curation effort, for other databases scores represent the importance of an activity.
