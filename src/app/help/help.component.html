Open Collaboration and Feedback

APICURON encourages open collaboration and welcomes user feedback to improve its functionality and usability. Users can contribute to the development of the database by providing suggestions, reporting bugs, or sharing their experiences. This collaborative approach ensures that the database meets the evolving needs of the user community and fosters continuous improvement.
You can share share your feedback directly By reporting it on: APICURON's issue tracker
By writing to: apicuron@ngp-net.bio.unipd.it
Or Joining the APICURON Slack
