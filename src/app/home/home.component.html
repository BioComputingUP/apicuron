Welcome to APICURON

APICURON provides biological databases with a real-time automatic tracking system of the activities of their biocurators, allowing them to properly credit their work. APICURON adopts the key concept of gamification, using a badge system and scores to acknowledge the effort of biocurators and to reward and encourage their engagement. In order to provide flexible and personalized badges, each partner database can define their own list of badges and rules to assign them.
